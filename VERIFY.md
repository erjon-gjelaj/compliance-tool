# Live-account verification register

The domain specification is based on consultancy material, not official
ISNetworld or Avetta documentation. Items below stay unknown or unverified in
config until checked against a live account. Do not fill gaps from memory.

## Phase 1: config

- Program lists produced by each supported platform for each trade and work
  type.
- The exact predicates behind “almost always,” “industrial sites,” and
  “commonly requested.”
- Hiring-client additions and removals from platform-generated lists.
- Whether the universal element list is complete for each platform and client.
- Every program-specific element list; section 4 calls it non-exhaustive.
- Official per-program submission questions, wording, ordering, and page-range
  behavior.
- Reviewer wording that reliably maps to R01 through R15.
- Platform and hiring-client grade weights. All remain null and unknown.
- Hiring-client grade thresholds and statistics thresholds. All remain null
  and unknown.
- Platform terminology outside the ISNetworld labels explicitly stated in the
  spec.

## Phase 2: extraction

- OCR provider/runtime limits and production accuracy on representative
  photographed binders.
- Multi-image ordering expectations in the current upload flow.

## Later phases

- Typical RAVS revalidation cadence and whether it varies by client.
- Whether one roster per topic and a toolbox talk are accepted in the relevant
  live account.
- Exact ACORD, 300A, EMR, and training upload slot behavior.
- Any public requirement-explorer combination before it is indexed.
